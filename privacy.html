Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Carefed ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Carefed app.

1. Information We Collect

Carefed collects only the information you voluntarily provide:

  • Profile information: age, household size, weekly budget, health conditions, dietary restrictions, and allergies — stored locally on your device only.
  • Location data: used only to find nearby grocery stores. We do not store or share your location.
  • Recipe preferences: saved recipes, star ratings, and filter preferences — stored locally on your device only.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not require account creation or login.
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, or phone numbers.
  • We do not track your activity across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell your data to third parties.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information you provide is used solely to:

  • Personalize recipe recommendations based on your budget, age, and health conditions.
  • Find grocery stores near your location.
  • Display relevant filters and suggestions within the app.

4. AI Chat Feature

When you use the AI Chat or Scan feature, your messages are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate recipe suggestions. Anthropic's privacy policy applies to this data. We do not store your chat history on our servers.

5. Third-Party Services

Carefed uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Places API – to find nearby grocery stores. Google Privacy Policy
  • Anthropic Claude API – to power AI recipe suggestions. Anthropic Privacy Policy
  • OpenStreetMap – to display maps. OSM Privacy Policy

6. Data Storage

All personal preferences (profile, saved recipes, ratings) are stored locally on your device using your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

7. Children's Privacy

Carefed is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
